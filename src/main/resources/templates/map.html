set
A ray stops where its distance plus the cost of the ground it has crossed exceeds the reach. Ground is a barrier only where it holds its height for 3 km (W-16), so a gully is crossed for nothing and the scarp is a wall; climbing costs what the slider says and descending its share of that, counted apart and added. A ray ends at the water - the sea or a lake at least 3 km across; a river is crossed - and a station with such water inside 10 km is coastal, held to the coastal limit on every bearing. A station the water would take three quarters of its rays from is an island: the water ends none of them, and it reaches across to the shore beyond.
